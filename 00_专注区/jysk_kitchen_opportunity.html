Style Trends Summary

JYSK's existing product range reveals a clear and consistent design language system.

🎨 Colour Palette

Beige — Base colour Apple Green — Freshness Dusty Grape — Premium feel Pale Khaki — Natural vibe Blue/White — Classic Nordic Floral — Decorative language

🧱 Core Materials

Stoneware — Texture leader Acacia Wood — Natural element Glass — Everyday主力 Natural Glaze — Nordic core

📐 Design Strategy Insights

  • ✦ Cross-category design reuse (EMIL floral motif extends from bowls to mugs and platters)
  • ✦ Clearance/discount for entry-level traffic (ELIAS -69%, FERDUS -33%), EDLP for premium lines to stabilise price expectations
  • ✦ Natural tones + stoneware = cost-efficient Nordic quality combination

06

Sales Opportunities & Development Directions

Based on comprehensive assessment of category coverage, price band distribution, and competitive landscape.

🫖

Tea Set Category Gap

JYSK has DETMER espresso/coffee cups and MATHIAS tea box, but lacks a complete teapot + cup & saucer set. This is a clear gap at the £15-25 mid-range, especially given the UK's stable tea culture demand.

High Potential
🧈

Breakfast / Butter Smallwares

Butter dishes, milk jugs, sugar bowls, egg cups — entirely missing. These are low unit price (£2-5), high margin items essential for building a "one-stop kitchen brand" perception.

High Potential
🍶

Stoneware Line Extension

MALIK and DETMER have proven stoneware acceptance. Adding pitchers, salad bowls, baking dishes in the same material system would strengthen the "stoneware matrix."

Medium Potential
🎄

Seasonal / Limited Editions

Currently only HILMER palm tree mug as seasonal. Christmas, spring floral, autumn earth tone themes would create urgency, drive repeat purchases, and work well as gift sets.

High Potential
📦

Gift Set Bundling

Current products are sold individually. Bundling EMIL floral bowl + mug + platter as a gift set could raise unit price from £7.50 to £10-12, a 30-50% increase. Ideal for holiday gifting.

High Potential
🪵

Natural Wood Series Expansion

SIGVART cutting board and MATHIAS tea box are the only two wood items. Adding wooden salad bowls, bread boards, utensil holders would create a complete "Natural Kitchen" sub-brand line that fits the Nordic aesthetic.

Medium Potential
🔪

Kitchen Tools Deepening

Cutting boards, trivets, and grinders exist, but measuring cups, mixing bowls, strainers, and bottle openers are missing. These low-price, high-frequency items drive cross-selling.

Medium Potential
🌍

Premium Line Branding

SIGURD and NOR series already occupy the £13.50-17.50 range but lack a clear "premium sub-brand" narrative. Dedicated naming and exclusive packaging could strengthen quality perception for gift/wedding markets.

Medium Potential
