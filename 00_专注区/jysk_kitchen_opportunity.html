Key Takeaways

VOLUME PLAY

56% of SKUs sit under £5. JYSK kitchenware is a low-ticket, high-volume business — margins depend on production efficiency and basket-building.

SWEET SPOT

£3 ~ £10 accounts for 63% of all SKUs. This is where material upgrades (stoneware, acacia, reactive glaze) unlock perceived value without crossing the £10 psychological threshold.

GAPS TO WATCH

£10 ~ £13 has only 1 SKU — a clear step-up gap. Kitchen textiles entirely absent. Cutlery only at £13.50. These are the most actionable whitespace for new development.

📐 AW27 Design Strategy

Extend Raw Beauty into Kitchen
AW27 target: 6~8 "Raw Table" SKUs. Reactive glazes in aubergine, old blue, petrol —speckled stoneware that echoes JYSK's SS26 Raw Beauty textile palette. Seagrass-trimmed serveware as the organic accent layer.
Named Collections, Not One-Offs
JYSK's furniture runs named collections (JONSTRUP, New Nordic Mood). Kitchen has 19 one-off series. Consolidate into 3 AW families: "Raw Table" (reactive glaze), "Nordic Kitchen" (matte neutrals), "Natural Kitchen" (wood).
🔄
Dual-Season Colour Strategy
Same forms, seasonal colours. Base neutrals (beige, pale khaki) run year-round EDLP. SS brings Renewing accents (apple green, dusty grape). AW brings Raw Beauty accents (aubergine, old blue). Minimal tooling, maximum seasonal freshness.
Anchor on Proven Bestsellers
EMIL (#1 Popular, 14 reviews) and FERDUS (37 reviews) are the trust anchors. AW novelties launch alongside —not instead of— these staples. Gift bundling (EMIL bowl + mug + platter) unlocks £10~£12 ASP without new product cost.

AW Development Directions

8 product development recommendations aligned with JYSK's seasonal trend system, core market preferences (DE/DK/Nordics), and identified portfolio gaps. Each direction maps to JYSK's SS/AW launch cadence —targeting AW27 and beyond.

🍇

AW "Raw Table" Collection

JYSK's SS26 "Raw Beauty" trend (aubergine, old blue, petrol blue, organic raw forms) has zero kitchen representation. Launch 6~8 AW kitchen SKUs under a named "Raw Table" collection: reactive-glaze bowls in aubergine + old blue, seagrass-trimmed serving platters, textured stoneware dinner plates. Extends the proven MALIK/NOR launch playbook into AW territory. Pure incremental revenue —no cannibalisation of SS colours.

High · AW27 Target
🫖

Tea & Coffee Set Launch

DETMER cups + MATHIAS tea box exist as solo items, but no complete teapot + cup & saucer set. The German and Danish markets have strong tea/coffee culture —this is the #1 missing category for core markets. Launch a 4-SKU set (teapot, 2 cups, saucers, sugar bowl) in matte stoneware at £15~£25. Align with either "Renewing" colours (dusty grape) or "Raw Beauty" (old blue) depending on launch window.

High · AW27 Priority
🧈

Breakfast Table Suite

Butter dish, milk jug, sugar bowl, egg cup —entirely absent. These are low-unit (£2~£5), high-margin items that complete the "full table" story. Use existing stoneware supply chain, launch in base neutrals (beige, pale khaki) for year-round placement, with seasonal colour accents (apple green for SS, aubergine for AW). 4~6 SKUs, minimal tooling cost, immediate cross-sell uplift with existing dinnerware.

High · AW27 Target
📦

Gift Sets from Proven Anchors

EMIL (#1 Plates Popular, 7~14 reviews per item) and FERDUS (37 reviews, #3 Popular) are customer-validated anchors. Bundle EMIL bowl + mug + platter → £10~£12 gift set (35~60% ASP uplift). FERDUS beige + grey dual-colour bowls → £8 "Nordic Duo" set. Launch in Q3 for AW/Christmas gifting window. Named collection packaging (matching JONSTRUP aesthetic) adds perceived value without product change.

High · AW27 Target
🌿

Consolidate into Named Kitchen Families

19 of 36 series are single-SKU —JYSK's furniture side uses named collections (JONSTRUP, New Nordic Mood) to consolidate and brand. Apply same model to kitchen: group existing products into 3~5 named families. "Nordic Table" (EMIL + FERDUS + BERTIL: everyday stoneware), "Raw Kitchen" (SIGURD + NOR: premium matte), "Natural Kitchen" (CLAES + SIGVART + MATHIAS: wood accessories). Reduces sourcing complexity and strengthens brand recognition in DE/DK markets.

High · AW27 Target
🍶

Stoneware Matrix: AW Deepening

MALIK (4 new SKUs, £6~£8) and DETMER (2 SKUs) anchor entry stoneware. FERDUS (5 SKUs, 37 reviews) validates the material. Extend into serving bowls, pitchers, and baking dishes —same clay body, same matte glazes, minimal tooling. For AW, add reactive-glaze variants in old blue and aubergine to test Raw Beauty colours in kitchen. 4~6 SKUs, £5~£15, dual SS/AW colour strategy.

Medium · AW27+
🪵

"Natural Kitchen" Wood Line

SIGVART (2 sizes), MATHIAS (#1 Accessories Popular), and EINAR (10 reviews) prove material-differentiated accessories drive engagement. JYSK's "Raw Beauty" trend uses seagrass and natural textures —extend into kitchen: wooden salad bowls, seagrass placemats, acacia serving boards. 4~5 SKUs, £5~£15, positioned as the "Raw Beauty" kitchen accessories companion. Cross-merchandise with existing wood items under one in-store bay.

Medium · AW27+
🌍

Premium Kitchen Sub-Brand for AW

9 SKUs above £10, ceiling at £17.50. JYSK's trend system creates natural premium positioning: AW "Raw Table" in reactive glazes at £15~£25 bridges the dead zone. Give the AW premium kitchen line a dedicated Nordic name (e.g. "VINTERKO" or "MORK") with gift-ready packaging. SIGURD (£13.50~£17.50) + new AW additions form the core. Target the £18~£30 wedding/gifting market in Germany and Scandinavia —a segment JYSK already serves in textiles and furniture.

Medium · AW28 Horizon

✅ What JYSK Kitchen Gets Right

  • ▸EDLP price leadership —£1.25 entry, core £3~£5. Undercuts IKEA/Dunelm across DE, DK, and UK markets on stoneware.
  • ▸Trend-aligned SS26 kitchen palette —MALIK and NOR already use Renewing colours (apple green, dusty grape). Kitchen is organically syncing with JYSK's formal trend system.
  • ▸Customer-validated anchors —FERDUS (37 reviews), GUNVALD (35 reviews), EMIL series (7~14 reviews) are proven bundling and cross-sell anchors.
  • ▸Active SS26 pipeline —14 new SKUs (MALIK 4, NOR 2, ISAK 2, +6 others). 24% new-product ratio signals investment in kitchen as a growth category.

⚠️ AW Development Priorities

  • ▸"Raw Beauty" kitchen gap —SS26's second trend (aubergine, old blue, petrol blue, organic raw forms) has no kitchen products. AW27 is the natural launch window for a "Raw Table" collection.
  • ▸£18~£30 dead zone —Ceiling at £17.50. AW reactive-glaze products in Raw Beauty colours can justify £15~£25. Wedding/gifting market inaccessible below this tier.
  • ▸19 one-off series —Half of all series are single-SKU. Kitchen should adopt JYSK's furniture model: 3~5 named collections per season, consolidating SKUs under brand identities.
  • ▸Missing "one-stop" kitchen —No tea sets, breakfast smallwares, kitchen textiles, or measuring tools. German discounters exploit this gap in JYSK's largest market.

🎯 AW Development Roadmap

  1. AW27 Development Inputs (H2 2026) —Launch breakfast smallwares in base neutrals (butter dish, milk jug, egg cup, sugar bowl: 4 SKUs, £2~£5). Bundle EMIL bowl + mug + platter as £10 gift set for Christmas. Package FERDUS beige + grey bowls as "Nordic Duo" set. All use existing stoneware supply chain —no new tooling required.
  2. AW27 Core Launch (July 2027) —"Raw Table" AW kitchen collection: 6~8 SKUs in reactive glazes (aubergine, old blue, petrol blue). Serving bowls, dinner plates, mugs —matching "Raw Beauty" trend palette. Tea set launch (teapot + 2 cups + saucers + sugar bowl, £15~£25) in old blue stoneware. Consolidate 19 one-off series into 3 named kitchen families. Total ~15 new SKUs.
  3. AW28 Horizon (2028) —Premium kitchen sub-brand (e.g. "VINTERKO") anchored on SIGURD + NOR + new AW reactive-glaze products at £15~£30. Gift-ready packaging, dedicated in-store bay in DE/DK flagships. Full seasonal kitchen rhythm: SS collection in Renewing colours, AW collection in Raw Beauty colours, with base neutrals running year-round EDLP. Three-tier matrix: Everyday Core (EDLP, £1~£10) + Seasonal Collection (SS/AW, £5~£20) + Premium Gifting (sub-brand, £15~£30).
